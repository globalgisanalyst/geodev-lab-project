# My Project Brief

### **1. My Project Question**

Which wards in Ogbomosho South LGA are more than 5km from a health facility?

### **## 2. Why It Matters**

Access to healthcare is influenced not only by whether health facilities exist, but also by their geographic proximity to the communities they serve. In Ogbomosho South LGA, health facilities are distributed across different wards, meaning some communities may be considerably farther from healthcare services than others. This project uses GIS to identify wards in Ogbomosho South LGA that are more than 5 km from a health facility. Identifying these potential geographic access gaps can help highlight areas that may warrant further investigation and could support evidence-based decisions about the location of future health facilities or outreach services. Ultimately, the project demonstrates how spatial data can be used to move from simply mapping the locations of health facilities to identifying potential gaps in healthcare coverage.

### **## 3. The Data I Need For This Project**

* GRID3 Health Facilities
* GRID3 Ward Boundaries
* OSM Roads

### **## 5. What I Will Build**

I will build a map highlighting potentially underserved wards and a summary of the wards identified as having limited geographic access to health facilities
